6 · Conductors

Inside: $E=0$, $\rho_v=0$, $V$ const; charge on surface. Surface: $E_t=0$, $D_n=\rho_s$, $E_n=\rho_s/\epsilon$

Plates: facing faces $\sigma_2=-\sigma_3$; outer $\sigma_1=\sigma_4=\dfrac{q_{tot}}{2S}$; grounded → outer faces 0

6e · Current & resistance

$I=\dfrac{dQ}{dt}=\displaystyle\int_S\vec J\cdot d\vec S$ · $\vec J=\rho_v\vec v$ · $\vec v_e=-\mu_e\vec E$ · $\vec J=\sigma\vec E$, $\sigma=-\rho_e\mu_e$ (S/m)

$\Delta V=EL$, $J=\sigma E=\dfrac IS$ ⇒ $V=IR$, $R=\dfrac{L}{\sigma S}$ · $\mu_e$: m²/(V·s), $\sigma$: S/m = 1/(Ω·m)

Circuit: $dv=R\,di$ · $dq=C\,dv$ · $dq=i\,dt$ ⇒ $i=C\,\dfrac{dv}{dt}$

With current flowing $E\ne0$ inside a conductor (not equipotential).

Recipes
  • Gauss: symmetry → surface where $D$ const & ⟂ → $Q_{enc}$ per region → $D$ → $E=D/\epsilon$.
  • C: assume $\pm Q$ → $D$ (Gauss) → $E$ → $V=-\int E\,dL$ → $C=Q/V$. R: assume $I$ → $J$ → $E$ → $V$ → $R=V/I$.
  • Work/V: pick the easiest path; $V$ is a scalar (no components). Check sign: $+Q$ moved against $\vec E$ ⇒ $W>0$.
  • Traps: $\vec R$ points source → P · $\rho$ = radius vs $\rho_v$ = charge · cm/mm/nC/μC · $V(\infty)=0$ not valid for infinite lines/sheets.
